Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy describes how the app Prezzi Carburanti ("the app") handles users' data. The app is developed by Luca De Leo, who can be contacted at luca.deleo@gmail.com.

In short

  • The app requires no registration and does not collect personal data on its own servers: it has no server.
  • Location is used only to search for nearby fuel stations and is neither stored nor shared for any other purpose.
  • There are no ads, analytics or tracking systems.

Location

The app asks for permission to access location only while in use (never in the background). Location is used to:

  • search for nearby stations: the coordinates of the search point are sent to the service of the Italian Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy (carburanti.mise.gov.it), which returns the stations in the area;
  • show the street or town name: the coordinates are passed to the operating system's geocoding service (on Android, normally provided by Google).

If permission is not granted the app still works: you can search for a town or an address, or pick a point on the map. In this case the searched text is passed to the operating system's geocoding service and the coordinates of the chosen place to the Ministry's service.

Data sent to third parties

The app communicates only with these services, and only for the stated purposes:

  • Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy: coordinates of the search point and radius (station search), station identifier (details), download of the open price data.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation: the map tiles of the displayed area; as with any website, the server receives the device's IP address.
  • Overpass API (overpass-api.de, a service that queries OpenStreetMap data), only if "Usa i dati di OpenStreetMap" (Use OpenStreetMap data) is on in the advanced settings: the coordinates of the stations found, to learn the type of road and area and estimate the travel time. The user's location is not sent; the server receives the device's IP address.
  • Operating system geocoding service: coordinates or searched text, to obtain place names and addresses.
  • Navigation app (e.g. Google Maps), only if you use "Portami qui" (Take me there): it receives the location of the chosen station.
  • App chosen for sharing, only if you use "Condividi" (Share): it receives the text with the station's data.

Each service processes data according to its own policy.

Data stored on the device

The app stores only on the device:

  • the chosen settings, including the car category;
  • the favourite stations;
  • a copy of the Ministry's open data (station registry and prices) and of the latest prices received, to work without a connection;
  • the type of road and area of the stations received from OpenStreetMap, to avoid requesting it on every search.

This data contains no personal information and is not sent to anyone. Downloaded data can be deleted at any time with "Svuota cache" (Clear cache) in the settings; everything is deleted by uninstalling the app.

Permissions required

  • Location (approximate and precise), only while in use: to search for nearby stations.
  • Internet access and network state: to download prices and, if set, to download the open data only over Wi-Fi.

Children

The app is not specifically aimed at children and does not knowingly collect data about them.
